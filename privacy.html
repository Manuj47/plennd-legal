Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

Plennd is operated by Manuj Arora ("we"). Plennd helps adults in Jaipur host and join small in-person plans. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Phone number: to create your account and log you in with a one-time code.
  • Name: you provide it. Other users see only your first name and last initial.
  • Location: when you check in, your phone's location is sent to our server to confirm you are at the venue. When you search for venues, your approximate location may be used to rank results. We use location only while the app is open, never in the background.
  • Activity: plans you create or join, requests, approvals, check-ins, ratings and votes, and Meet Credits earned and spent.
  • Messages: messages between the host and a joiner of the same plan.
  • Reports and blocks: reports you file and people you block.
  • Age confirmation: the time you confirmed you are 18 or older.
  • Technical data: IP address and request logs kept by our hosting provider, used for security and abuse prevention.

We do not collect your contacts, photos or government ID numbers.

Why we use it

To run the service (accounts, plans, check-in), to keep people safe (reports, moderation, suspending abusive accounts), to prevent fraud and abuse (limits on one-time codes and repeat free plans), and to meet legal duties. We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for advertising.

Who sees it

  • Other users: your first name and last initial, your rating, and plan details for plans you host or join. Your phone number is never shown. Messages are visible only to you and the other person in the conversation.
  • Hosting: Railway, with servers in the United States.
  • Venue search: Open Places API receives the search text and approximate location from our server, not your identity.
  • One-time codes: our SMS provider (our SMS provider) receives your phone number.
  • Payments: when paid plans launch, a payment provider handles your payment details. We do not store card or UPI details.
  • Authorities: where the law requires it.

Because our servers are outside India, your data is processed outside India.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account we delete your name, phone number, messages, blocks and any verification requests, and anonymise the rest. The Meet Credits balance is removed on deletion and anonymised credit records are kept for financial records. We keep anonymised records of plans, joins, ratings, payouts, credit records and safety reports, plus a one-way hash of your phone number, to prevent fraud, enforce suspensions and keep financial records. One-time codes expire within minutes.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Delete your account in the app under Profile, or see how to delete your account. For anything else, email manujarora47@gmail.com.

Security

We use encrypted connections and limit who can access data. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Age

Plennd is for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we delete it.

Complaints

Grievance Officer: Manuj Arora, manujarora47@gmail.com. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above, and tell you in the app about material changes.
