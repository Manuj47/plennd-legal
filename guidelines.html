Community Guidelines

Last updated 13 October 2026

Plennd works when people feel safe and show up. These rules apply to everyone.

Be honest and show up

  • Use your real first name and be who you say you are.
  • If you can't make it, cancel early so someone else can take your spot.

Respect other people

  • No harassment, unwelcome advances, threats or hate.
  • No discrimination based on religion, caste, gender, disability or background.
  • No sexual content, and no illegal activity.
  • Do not record or photograph people without their consent, or share their personal details.
  • Do not use Plennd to sell or promote anything.

Keep yourself safe

  • Meet in public places and stay in public places.
  • Tell a friend where you are going and who you are meeting.
  • Do not feel pressured to share your number, move to another app, or pay outside Plennd.
  • Trust your instincts. You can leave at any time.
  • In an emergency, call 112.

Report and block

You can report or block anyone from a plan or a chat. We review reports, and we never tell the person who reported them.

What happens if rules are broken

We may warn you, hold rewards, suspend you, or remove you permanently. Where the law requires it, we share information with the police.
