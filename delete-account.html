Last updated 13 October 2026

In the app

Open Plennd, go to Profile, tap Delete account and confirm. If you host or have joined a plan that is open, full or in progress, cancel it first.

By email

If you can't open the app, email manujarora47@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Plennd account" and the phone number on the account. We will confirm it is you, for example by sending a one-time code to that number, and delete the account within 30 days.

What we delete

Your name, phone number, messages, blocked-user list and any verification requests. You are logged out everywhere.

What we keep

Anonymised records of plans, joins, ratings, rewards and safety reports, and a one-way hash of your phone number. We keep these to prevent fraud, enforce suspensions and keep financial records. They no longer identify you.
